## 1. THE FEATURE
**Name:** Placement Power-Up
**One-line pitch:** Unlock hidden job opportunities and bypass college placement rules.

button: "Scan My College Placement Policy".
10-25s: User taps "Scan My College Placement Policy". A prompt appears: "Upload your college's official placement policy document (PDF or image)." User uploads a PDF. A loading spinner with text "Analyzing 100+ pages of legalese..." appears.
25-45s: The spinner disappears. A bold, red banner flashes: "**POLICY VIOLATION DETECTED!** Your college's 'one job rule' is preventing you from applying to [Company X, Y, Z] (30LPA+)." A share button with a WhatsApp icon appears prominently: "Share your unlocked potential with your batchmates!"

## 3. THE SHAREABLE ARTIFACT
The shareable artifact is a dynamic, personalized image generated by KodeTalent, designed for instant virality on WhatsApp. It features:
- **What's on it?** A sleek, minimalist card with the user's name and photo (if available), a bold headline like "**I just bypassed the 'one job rule' for a 30LPA+ offer!**", and a blurred background of a prestigious company logo (e.g., Google, Microsoft). Below, in smaller text, it says "Powered by KodeTalent's Placement Power-Up." A QR code is subtly placed in the corner, linking directly to the KodeTalent app download page with the Placement Power-Up feature highlighted. The overall aesthetic is aspirational and exclusive, using a dark mode theme with neon green accents.
- **Why is it impossible to scroll past?** The headline is a direct, provocative challenge to a universally hated college policy. The combination of a high LPA figure and the claim of bypassing a restrictive rule creates immediate intrigue and FOMO. The blurred company logo adds an element of mystery and prestige, making recipients want to know more.
- **Who exactly is it forwarded to first?** Hostel group, batch group, close friends struggling with placements, and perhaps even parents (to show progress and alleviate pressure).
- **What does the recipient feel? What do they do next?** Recipients feel a mix of shock, envy, and urgent curiosity. "Yeh bhi possible hai??" (Is this even possible??) will be the immediate thought. They will instantly click the QR code or ask the sender for details, leading them directly to the KodeTalent app to try the feature themselves. They will also likely discuss it in their groups, amplifying the buzz.

## 4. THE VIRAL LOOP (k-factor mechanism)
- **Step 1: User does X** A 3rd-year B.Tech student, frustrated by the college's restrictive "one job rule" and the lack of opportunities for high-paying roles, discovers KodeTalent's "Placement Power-Up" feature. They upload their college's placement policy document.
- **Step 2: App produces shareable artifact Y** KodeTalent's AI analyzes the policy, identifies the restrictive clauses, and generates a personalized "Policy Violation Detected" card. It then presents a "Bypass Policy (Stealth Mode)" option, leading to a pre-filled application for a high-paying company that would otherwise be inaccessible. Upon successful application, a shareable image is generated: "I just bypassed the 'one job rule' for a 30LPA+ offer! Powered by KodeTalent's Placement Power-Up."
- **Step 3: User shares to N friends because [psychological reason]** The user shares this artifact to their hostel and batch WhatsApp groups due to a powerful combination of **Identity flex** ("look at this thing about ME") and **FOMO trigger** ("if I don't share, friends will get the edge"). The sheer audacity of bypassing a universally disliked college rule, coupled with the aspiration of a high-paying job, makes it an irresistible share. It's a badge of honor and a secret weapon.
- **Step 4: Friends see, click, land on app** Friends in the WhatsApp groups see the provocative image. The headline and the implied success immediately grab their attention. The QR code or direct link embedded in the image leads them to the KodeTalent app, specifically to the Placement Power-Up feature.
- **Step 5: Friends are FORCED to engage to [reciprocate / compete / not feel left out]** Upon landing in the app, friends are presented with the same "Scan My College Placement Policy" option. The fear of being left behind, coupled with the desire to gain the same advantage as their peer, compels them to engage with the feature. They want to see if their college policy can also be bypassed and if they too can access these hidden opportunities. The social comparison aspect is extremely strong here.
- **Step 6: Loop restarts** Each new user who successfully bypasses their college policy and applies for a job generates their own shareable artifact, restarting the loop.

**Estimate: realistic k-factor for a B.Tech batch of 60 students.** Given the intense competition and pressure during placement season, and the universal frustration with restrictive college policies, a k-factor of **1.5-2.0** is realistic. If one student shares with 10 friends, and 1.5-2.0 of those friends convert and share, the growth would be exponential within a batch. The novelty and direct benefit (high-paying job access) would drive rapid adoption.

"Most people think that the primary challenge for Indian engineering students in securing high-paying jobs lies in their technical skills, resume quality, or interview preparation. The truth is, for a significant portion of ambitious students, the most debilitating obstacle is not their capability, but the archaic and restrictive college placement policies, particularly the universally loathed 'one job rule,' which actively curtails their access to superior opportunities and fosters a culture of fear and missed potential [1]. Therefore, 'Placement Power-Up' is the inevitable wedge, directly addressing this systemic bottleneck by empowering students to reclaim agency over their career paths."

## 8. WHY NO COMPETITOR HAS BUILT IT
(b) "No competitor has built this because [structural reason — wrong team type, wrong distribution, wrong incentive, regulatory friction]. We CAN build it because [our specific unfair advantage]."

No competitor has built this because existing players like Internshala and Superset are deeply integrated with and incentivized by the colleges and their TPOs. Their business model relies on maintaining the status quo and facilitating placements *within* the existing college framework. Building a feature that actively helps students bypass college policies would be a direct conflict of interest and would jeopardize their relationships with educational institutions. KodeTalent, on the other hand, is student-first and AI-native, allowing us to operate outside these traditional constraints. Our unfair advantage is our direct-to-student distribution and our ability to leverage AI (Claude) to interpret and exploit policy loopholes without human bias or institutional allegiance.

## 9. WHY IT FITS KODETALENT SPECIFICALLY
This feature becomes 10x more powerful because KodeTalent already has the **AI chat** capability to process and understand complex policy documents, and the **recruiter portal** to directly connect students with companies outside the TPO-controlled ecosystem. The existing **KodeScore** can be enhanced to reflect a student's 'policy bypass potential,' further gamifying the experience and reinforcing the identity flex.
